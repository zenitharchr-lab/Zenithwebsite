About

HR support that behaves like part of your team — not an outside vendor.

Zenith Arc HR is a freelance HR practice built on hands-on experience across recruitment, payroll operations, policy design and statutory compliance since 2024.

We work with small and growing businesses that need dependable HR support without the overhead of building a full in‑house team — stepping in on exactly the pieces you need, whether that's a single hiring mandate or ongoing payroll and compliance.
